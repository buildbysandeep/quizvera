# Quizvera

**Create. Share. Assess. Improve.**

Quizvera is a modern quiz management platform that makes it easy to create assessments, collaborate with others, manage participants, and track performance—all in one place.

## Features

- **Quiz Management:** Create, edit, duplicate, publish, and organize quizzes into groups.
- **Collaboration:** Share quizzes with other Quizvera users and manage quiz administrators.
- **Question Bank:** Create and manage questions, with AI-assisted question generation.
- **Flexible Timers:** Configure individual quiz timers, automatic closing, manual starts, and scheduled availability.
- **Participant Management:** View submissions by date, manage participants, and review individual answers.
- **Results & Analytics:** Track scores, qualification status, participation, and quiz performance.
- **Reusable Image Library:** Upload, organize, and reuse images across quizzes.
- **Personal Quiz History:** Access completed quizzes and review your results.
- **Dashboard:** Get an overview of your quizzes, groups, submissions, and recent activity.
- **Profile Management:** Update your name and profile picture.

Quizvera helps educators, trainers, and organizations **create engaging assessments, collaborate efficiently, and understand learning outcomes.**
